Gestures
✋ Open / close hand: explode / assemble
👋 Move one hand: the model follows it
🙌 Two hands: spread apart = zoom in, bring together = zoom out
🤏 Pinch a part: inspect it (pinch empty space to clear)
👈 Swipe one hand right → left: next model
Mouse: click a part · N / P = next / previous · Esc = clear
